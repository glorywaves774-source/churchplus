# ChurchPlus – guide for churches

A plain-language tour for church administrators, pastors and leaders.

## Getting started
1. **Register your church** at the sign-up page. You become the *Church Owner* and get a free trial.
2. **Church settings**: add your address, phone, email and logo. These appear on receipts, the
   website and messages.
3. **Branches** (if you have more than one assembly) and **Users & roles**: invite your team and give
   each person only the roles they need (e.g. *Accountant*, *Membership Officer*, *Counsellor*).
4. **Members**: add people one by one or import a spreadsheet (Members → Import).
5. **Install the app**: on your phone choose *Install app* (Android) or *Share → Add to Home Screen*
   (iPhone). It opens full screen like any other app.

## Everyday work
| I want to… | Go to |
|---|---|
| Record a new member or first-timer | Members → Add member · Visitors → New first-timer |
| Take attendance | Attendance → choose the service → tick, scan QR cards or type numbers |
| Record tithes and offerings | Giving & finance → Record giving (a numbered receipt is created) |
| Pay a bill | Giving & finance → Expenses → New voucher (someone else approves it) |
| See the accounts | Accounting → trial balance, income and expenditure, balance sheet, cash flow |
| Plan a programme | Events → New event (registration, waiting list, budget, committee) |
| Share a sermon | Sermons & media → Add sermon, upload the recording, choose who can see it |
| Follow up prayer requests | Prayer (confidential ones only reach the assigned person and pastors) |
| Pastoral counselling | Counselling (notes are seen by the counsellor only) |
| Sunday school check-in | Children & youth → child → Check in (prints two labels with the same code) |
| Text or email members | Communication → New message → choose SMS, WhatsApp or email and the group |
| Post a notice | Communication → Announcements (staff, members, or everyone on the website) |
| Get a report | Reports → choose report and period → Print, CSV, Excel or PDF |
| Turn on the website | Church website → tick *The website is live* |
| Pay ChurchPlus | Subscription → open the invoice → Pay now (Paystack or Hubtel) |

## Member portal
On a member's record choose **Invite to member portal** (they need an email address). They get
an email to choose a password, then see: announcements, events (and can register), sermons,
their own giving statement and pledges, prayer requests, their family, and can update their
contact details and message preferences. Portal sign-ins do not count towards your plan's users.

## Messages and reminders
* Messages are personalised: `{first_name}`, `{name}`, `{church}`. Preview shows how many people
  will receive it and how many SMS parts it uses.
* Members who opted out of SMS or email are skipped automatically. WhatsApp goes only to people
  who agreed to it.
* Communication → Settings: your SMS sender name, automatic **birthday greetings**, and
  reminders the day before events and counselling appointments (the text never says what the
  appointment is about).

## Privacy and safety
* People see only what their roles allow. Hidden records simply do not appear.
* Counselling: a case is visible to its counsellor; standard cases also to pastors with
  oversight; *restricted* cases only to the counsellor. Notes are counsellor-only. Every opening
  is recorded in the audit trail.
* Children: only authorised guardians may collect, with the matching pick-up code. Wrong codes
  are refused and recorded. Safeguarding concerns are visible only to people with approval rights.
* The Church Owner can download all church data (Church settings → Download your data);
  counselling, safeguarding and confidential prayer are not included.
* ChurchPlus staff manage subscriptions only. They cannot open your members, giving, prayer,
  counselling or children's records.

## If something goes wrong
The error page shows a short reference (for example `3f9c1a7b2d4e5f60`). Send it to ChurchPlus
support so we can find exactly what happened.
